## Hi there 👋

I'm a student excited to learn how data can help us understand people, performance, and the world around us.

### 🌱 What I'm learning

- **Data analysis:** spreadsheets, Python, SQL, and exploratory data analysis
- **Statistics:** descriptive statistics, distributions, correlation, and hypothesis testing
- **Visualization:** turning data into clear charts and useful dashboards
- **Machine learning:** predicting outcomes and understanding which factors matter
- **Responsible data use:** data quality, privacy, reproducibility, and avoiding misleading conclusions

### 🏃 What interests me

I am especially interested in **endurance sports** such as running, cycling, and swimming. Sports data makes learning data science practical and motivating. I want to explore questions such as:

- How does running pace change with distance, terrain, and weather?
- Is training load related to fatigue or injury risk?
- How do heart rate, perceived effort, and recovery explain performance?
- How could a training schedule be adjusted for a realistic goal?

Some project ideas I would like to try are cleaning training logs, visualizing my workout progress, comparing personal results over time, and building a small model to estimate race performance or recovery.
